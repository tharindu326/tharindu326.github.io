MacDon Industries · Agricultural vision

Client project

Nov 2022 – May 2024 · Jan 2026 – May 2026 · Contract via ZOOMi · macdon.com

Computer vision for the R&D group of a harvesting-equipment manufacturer. The models give combine harvesters real-time awareness of the crop and the machine: where the rows are, what state the crop is in, how clean the cut is and what is around the vehicle, to support automated guidance and control during harvest.

  • Crop-row guidance: Trained U-Net segmentation models that find corn and soybean rows in real and synthetic field images, then extracted each row's center line (position and angle) so the harvester can steer along the rows.
  • Crop stage and condition: Built classifiers for crop lodging stage (partial, severe, full) and lodging direction, and segmentation and detection models that separate standing, lodged and cut crop and tire tracks in wheat fields.
  • Cut quality: Trained RF-DETR detection and instance-segmentation models that find harvest defects in wheat and canola, such as streaking, flagging, pushing and missed or damaged crop.
  • Header monitoring: Trained a ViT-B/16 model to assess the state of corn header parts (auger, row units and snouts) at low, moderate and high levels.
  • Field awareness: Detected grain carts, trailers, tractors, trucks and combines around the harvester, comparing RF-DETR and YOLOv12 detectors.
  • From field data to deployment: Simulated field dust, blur, noise and lighting during training, evaluated models with per-class mAP, F1, confusion matrices and latency profiling, and deployed them through ONNX and TensorRT FP16. Owned the data pipeline from the LayerNext data lake and S3 through to the trained model.

Computer-vision backend for a surgical analytics product. Multiple operating-room cameras stream into a multi-process pipeline that detects and tracks staff and equipment, turns their movement into workflow events and pushes them live to clinical dashboards over ZeroMQ and WebSockets. Largest contributor to the vision codebase.

  • Multi-process pipeline: Worked as part of the team on a ZeroMQ pipeline in which every camera, model and tracker runs as its own process, with detectors replicated across multiple GPUs and frames load-balanced between them.   • Detection: Wrote the PyTorch (YOLOv5, YOLOv7), OpenCV DNN and TensorRT inference backends and moved the detector across YOLO generations, then ported the TensorRT detector into the later job-queue architecture (Faktory and Redis) running on multiple GPU containers.
  • Tracking and events: Brought in and tuned ByteTrack, whose second association pass recovers low-confidence detections of partly hidden staff, and built zones and boundary lines anchored by ArUco markers that turn tracks into events: entering and leaving areas such as the surgical table or floor, and live counts per region.
  • Multi-camera 3D tracking: Contributed to 3D tracking from a calibrated camera pair, which matches detections across views, triangulates them into 3D and filters out false "ghost" matches. I worked on the 3D tracker and 3D event logic, the 3D zones (table, floor, Mayo stand) and the top-down view streamed to the dashboard.
  • Efficiency and privacy: Added a background-subtraction motion stage that skips detection on static frames and only processes moving regions, plus automatic blurring of faces and name tags.

Research & Open Source

Projects from my research at the University of Oulu and open-source work.

Open source · 2025 – Present

PhysioTrack

An open-source Python library (GPL-3.0) for contactless human understanding. The idea is to send meaning, not pixels: it turns ordinary RGB video into compact physiological and behavioral signals that people and AI systems can reason about, for healthcare, education, XR training and operator support.

  • Physiological signals: rPPG heart rate (POS, CHROM, LGI, OMIT), HRV and respiration, validated against NeuroKit2
  • Body and face: 2D/3D pose with 3DPCNet canonicalization, clinical joint angles and range of motion, head pose, gaze, blinks and expression
  • Nearly 60 pretrained models (YOLO11/12, RT-DETR, ViTPose, Sapiens, MotionBERT, Depth-Anything-V2, SegFace) and four trackers behind one predictor API, with weights on Hugging Face
  • Released through semantic-release CI, with MkDocs documentation and about 230 tests

Research · ICASSP 2026

3DPCNet: viewpoint-invariant 3D pose

Monocular 3D pose estimates depend on where the camera is. 3DPCNet maps any 3D pose into a consistent body-centered frame so that kinematic analysis holds across viewpoints and environments.

  • Hybrid GCN-Transformer with adaptive graph learning and cross-attention fusion
  • 6D rotation representation for stable canonicalization
  • ~35% lower MPJPE (74.1 → 48.4 mm) in cross-environment evaluation
arXiv Code
